BrainChip Connect is the companion app for BrainChip development boards built
around the AKD1500 accelerator, including the AkidaTag and the BrainBoard1500.
It connects over Bluetooth Low Energy and puts everything the board can tell
you on the phone in your hand.

There is no server behind it. The app has no backend, no account and no network
permission in a released build. It talks to nothing but your board.

FIND AND CONNECT
Scan for boards nearby and connect with one tap. The app recognises BrainChip
hardware by the accelerator id the board advertises, so it finds your board
whatever that board happens to be named. Once connected you can read its serial
number, firmware version, Bluetooth protocol and battery level.

RUN AN APPLICATION
See the applications your board carries and what each one is: the model it runs,
the shape of its input, how many classes it knows and, for keyword spotting, the
exact list of words it listens for. Start one with Run Application and the app
loads that model into the Akida accelerator, showing you the wait while the
board swaps it in.

WATCH RESULTS AS THEY HAPPEN
Keyword spotting shows the word the board heard and how confident it was, beside
a live trace of the microphone the board is listening on. Human detection shows
what the camera found, with a live preview image streamed from the board itself.
Every reading is the board's own. The app draws what it is sent and invents
nothing.

TUNE AND TEACH
Adjust the detector running on the board without rebuilding its firmware: RMS
threshold, debounce time, smoothing, score threshold, chiming threshold and
speech timeout, applied to the board and put back to their defaults whenever you
want. Switch on Edge Learning and teach the board a new sound on the spot.

KEEP A RECORD
The app keeps an event history of what each board reported, so you can look back
over what happened while you were not watching.

UPDATE OVER THE AIR
Update board firmware and swap the AI model over Bluetooth, from a package you
pick out of your phone's own storage. Each update reports what the board
actually did with it: whether the file was stored and verified, and whether the
new model was programmed into the accelerator and proven to infer. A model that
is stored but will not run is reported as exactly that, rather than as a
success.

PRIVACY
No analytics, no crash reporting, no telemetry, no account and no server. A
released build declares no internet permission at all, so the app cannot open a
network connection even if something asked it to. It holds three permissions:
Bluetooth scan, Bluetooth connect and notifications. Nothing you do in the app
is collected, shared or sold.

WHAT YOU NEED
A BrainChip board carrying the AKD1500 accelerator, and an Android phone running
Android 13 or later with Bluetooth switched on.
